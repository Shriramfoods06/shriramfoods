About Shri Ram Foods

Shri Ram Foods is a Nagpur-based food manufacturing and trading enterprise committed to standard food quality, authentic traditional flavor, and maximum freshness.

We specialize in authentic handcrafted snacks including multiple regional varieties of crunchy Bhakarwadi, savory Sev & Chivda mixes, and festive snacks. Additionally, we manufacture premium ultra-fine Rice Flour engineered for snacks preparation, baking, and bulk commercial food processing.
