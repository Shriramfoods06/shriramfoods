About Shri Ram Foods

Established in 2023, Shri Ram Foods operates as a dedicated food manufacturing and wholesale facility based in Kalamna, Nagpur. We cater to grocery stores, food marts, distributors, and party caterers across Maharashtra and neighboring regions.

Our facility specializes in authentic Maharashtrian and North Indian snacks—from specialty Bhakarwadi to crisp Mathri, flavorful Chivda mixtures, and golden crunchy Bundi. Every product is produced under hygienic supervision conforming to statutory quality norms.

Packaging & Quality Standards

Engineered for shelf longevity and bulk logistics

📦 Bulk Packaging Modes

  • Corrugated Master Boxes: 10 kg, 20 kg & 30 kg heavy-duty boxes lined with food-grade plastic.
  • Pre-Packed Pouches: 200g, 400g and 1kg heat-sealed retail pouches.
  • Rice Flour / Powder: 25kg & 50kg industrial woven poly sacks.

🛡️ Shelf Life & Storage

  • Standard Shelf Life: 90 Days from the manufacturing date.
  • Optimal Storage: Store in a cool, dry area away from direct sunlight & damp walls.
  • Re-sealing: Once opened, transfer to airtight commercial tins or containers.
