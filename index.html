About Shri Ram Foods

Shri Ram Foods is a trusted food manufacturing and trading enterprise based in Nagpur, Maharashtra. We are dedicated to delivering premium, hygienic, and authentic Indian traditional snacks prepared using quality ingredients.

Our manufacturing lineup features crispy Special Bhakarwadi, savory Sev, crunchy Chivda, Namakpara, and Shakkapara, alongside ultra-fine Rice Flour suitable for both industrial food processing and retail packing. We cater to wholesale distributors, retail partners, and bulk commercial orders with consistent quality and competitive market pricing.
